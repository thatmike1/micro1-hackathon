§ 1What is being measured

A case is proved when the agent's own test exits non-zero on the patched checkout and zero on the pristine one, under the library's own runner. The primary metric is not that rate on one run: it is the count of cases proved in every one of three repetitions, because a single draw hid the headroom — baseline 1 on flash was reported at 12/12 from one run and holds 7/12 across three (row 0f). Single-run rates are printed beside the primary number, never instead of it.

False alarms are printed per repetition. A defect claimed on an equivalent refactor is treated as a hard error, and averaging three draws of a metric whose target is zero would report a number the project never claims. Where a control was left without a verdict at all, that is said separately: an unanswered control is not a control that came back correct.

The two engines are measured over different cases and never share a table. flash covers all 15 cases (12 buggy + 3 controls); qwen covers the 12 ms+bytes cases (10 buggy + 2 controls). A rate over one slice is not a rate over the other, and no comparison on this page crosses between them. The shipped configuration is per engine for the same reason: stage 2 on flash, stage 1 on qwen.

Stage 3 is an on/off ablation and is scored against its own memory-off arm. Both off arms re-measure an unchanged candidate under a sequential case order and land one case below the row they re-measure, so reading the on arm against rows 1 and 2 would charge that gap to the memory.
